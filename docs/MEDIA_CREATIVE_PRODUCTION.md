# Media & Creative Production Capability

## LongCat-Video

LongCat-Video is retained as a planned video-generation capability in the Media & Creative Production department. It is **not installed, connected, or active** in the clean-room core.

The official model setup requires a CUDA-capable environment, PyTorch and model-specific dependencies, and downloaded model weights. It supports text-to-video and image-to-video workflows; local execution is not a phone-only task.

## Activation sequence

1. Inventory available compute and storage without launching paid resources.
2. Confirm the exact model licence and commercial-use conditions.
3. Identify an existing zero-cost GPU environment or a free allocation with hard limits.
4. Create an isolated worker adapter and test with a small non-commercial sample.
5. Record runtime, memory, storage, quality and cost evidence.
6. Keep generated files in review-required draft state.
7. Require owner approval for paid compute, subscriptions, publishing or customer delivery.

The capability registry intentionally reports researched_not_connected until these gates pass. No automatic fallback to paid video APIs is permitted.
